How to fly it

  1. Take-off: check flaps 1+F (press F), spoilers armed, autobrake MAX. Release the parking brake (P). Set thrust to FLX (3). Follow the runway centreline with Q/E. At “Rotate” pull back gently (~3°/s) to about 12.5° and follow the green flight-director bars.
  2. Climb: “Positive climb” → gear up (G). At LVR CLB flashing move the levers to CL (2): autothrust takes over. Engage the autopilot (Z) above 100 ft. Retract flaps on schedule (F / S speeds).
  3. Cruise: the FMS flies the great-circle route (NAV) and climbs to the FCU altitude. Speed up time with T.
  4. Descent: at T/D set a lower FCU altitude (End) and push the altitude knob (U). Use the speed brake if the FMA says MORE DRAG.
  5. Approach: press APPR (A) when cleared; flaps 1–2–3 on schedule, gear down, flaps FULL, arm spoilers (Shift+/) and autobrake (K). Autoland, or disconnect the AP (Z) and hand-fly the ILS.
  6. Landing: at “Fifty… thirty…” flare gently, at “RETARD” pull the thrust to idle (1). Reverse (R) and brake.

Phones & tablets: left pad = sidestick, right slider = thrust levers (snaps to IDLE, CL, FLX, TOGA), buttons for gear, flaps, speed brake, autopilot, reverse, brakes and steering. Drag the view to look around; ▦ shows the instruments. Gamepads & joysticks work too — a joystick’s trigger is the sidestick takeover (AP disconnect) button.
